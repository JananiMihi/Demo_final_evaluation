Cardiovascular Signals as Proxies for Skeletal Muscle Volume

HRVBP-HyperNet is a research framework that estimates muscle volumes from wearable cardiovascular data — heart rate variability captured via chest strap and serial blood pressure readings — validated against MRI-segmented ground truth.

Participants completed structured rest and exercise sessions in gym environments while CV signals were recorded. Predictions are produced through PCA-reduced HRV/BP features and a linear mixed-effects model trained on the MUSCAST cohort.

Technical Architecture

From Raw Signals to Muscle Volume

A five-stage pipeline transforms wearable cardiovascular signals into per-muscle volume predictions using dimensionality reduction and mixed-effects modelling.

01

RR Intervals

Polar H10 beat-to-beat intervals. REST + EXERCISE phases split by beat count.

02

BP Readings

Resting, post-exercise, and exercise series SBP/DBP. 19 derived variability features.

03

44 HRV Features

Time · Frequency · Nonlinear · RQA metrics. Log-transformed and standardised.

04

PCA Projection

Trained StandardScaler + 17 HR PCs + 8 BP PCs. 9 PCs enter the LME formula.

05

LME Prediction

Fixed-effects formula. Back-transform z-score → cm³. All 8 muscles at once.

Research Milestones

What We Accomplished

Key innovations and measurable outcomes from the HRVBP-HyperNet project.

RST-UNet V2
Novel Segmentation Architecture
Residual Shrinkage Blocks, MAM skip connections, Parallel Transformer Stream, and FiLM conditioning — achieving Dice = 0.7983 on MRI muscle segmentation.
44 Features
End-to-End HRV Pipeline
Automated computation of time-domain, frequency-domain, nonlinear, and RQA metrics from raw Polar H10 RR interval streams with phase-aware processing.
CCC 0.509
Non-Invasive Validation
Cardiovascular wearable signals achieve statistically significant concordance with ground-truth MRI muscle volumes at a fraction of the cost of imaging studies.
78 Participants
Multi-Group Dataset
Structured data collection across 4 distinct activity groups with rest and exercise protocols, paired with full-body MRI muscle volume ground truth.
HR_PC9
Key Predictor Identified
HR_PC9 (β=−0.116, p<0.01) and Gender_bin (β=0.621, p<0.001) are the strongest predictors. LF/HF, DFA α₁, and SD1/SD2 carry detectable muscle-volume signal.
Live Demo
Real-Time Web Application
Full-stack Flask app with Web Bluetooth integration — connect a Polar H10 live, record HRV in-browser, and receive immediate muscle volume predictions via the trained LME model.
