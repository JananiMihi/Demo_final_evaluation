44 HRV Features

Time · Frequency · Nonlinear · RQA metrics. Log-transformed and standardised.

Trained StandardScaler + 17 HR PCs + 8 BP PCs. 9 PCs enter the LME formula.

Fixed-effects formula. Back-transform z-score → cm³. All 8 muscles at once.

About

HRVBP-HyperNet Framework

🏛️ University of Ruhuna — EG/2021

Balasooriya J.M. · Bandara K.M.T.O.N. · Bandara L.R.T.D. · Bulegodaarachchi D.C.

🧠 RST-UNet V2 Architecture

Residual Shrinkage Blocks · MAM skip connections · Parallel Transformer Stream · FiLM conditioning. Dice=0.7983.

📡 Input Devices

Polar H10 chest strap — RR intervals · OMRON HEM-7121 — systolic / diastolic BP across rest, exercise, recovery phases.

🎯 Key Finding

HR_PC9 (β=−0.116, p<0.01) and Gender_bin (β=0.621, p<0.001) are the strongest predictors. LF/HF, DFA α₁, SD1/SD2 geometry carry detectable muscle-volume signal.
